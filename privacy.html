personal-agent privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

personal-agent is used only by its owner, the GitHub user lexlapax, with the owner's own Google account. No one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads the owner's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events through Google's APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to give the owner summaries, reminders and drafts. It is stored on the owner's own computers. Parts of it may be sent to an AI model provider chosen by the owner, only to produce those results for the owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

personal-agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored copies from those computers.

Contact
